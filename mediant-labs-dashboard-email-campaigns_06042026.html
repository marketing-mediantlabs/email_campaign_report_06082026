Key Observations & Recommendations

💡 Strategic Insights — June 2026 Email Campaigns

  • Metal & Mining is the highest quality lead source — Despite the smallest list (317 sent), Mining produced 44 leads at a 13.88% lead conversion rate, far outpacing Energy (3.75%) and Manufacturing (4.30%). The safety-focused subject line is highly resonant with this audience.
  • 155 total leads generated across 2,983 emails sent — Blended lead rate of 5.2%. Manufacturing contributed the most in volume (86 leads), Mining the most in efficiency, and Energy the least in both.
  • Mining's 47.27% open rate is the highest across all three — Safety and skills gap messaging is clearly compelling. Consider A/B testing a similar angle for the Energy audience in the next wave.
  • Manufacturing has the best read quality (36% Read rate) — More than one-third of openers fully read the email. Energy and Mining audiences skim more — consider shorter, punchier copy for those segments.
  • Unmailable contacts (285 combined) need immediate cleanup — 72 Energy + 194 Manufacturing + 19 Mining are blocked or bounced. These include hard bounces that permanently damage sender reputation if not removed before the next send.
  • Unsubscribe numbers are heavy in Manufacturing (864) and Energy (310) — Combined 1,283 unsubscribes across 2,983 sends (43%). This signals list quality or relevance mismatch. Segment by job title and company size before the next campaign.
  • Unopened contacts are the biggest re-engagement opportunity — 355 (Energy) + 1,224 (Manufacturing) + 164 (Mining) = 1,743 unopened. A follow-up with a different subject line targeting only this segment could unlock significant additional leads at near-zero cost.
  • Calendly booking links perform consistently across all 3 campaigns — 15 unique clicks (Energy), 34 (Manufacturing), 10 (Mining). Consolidating MS Bookings (Energy, 8 clicks) into Calendly as the single CTA would reduce friction and improve meeting conversion tracking.
  • Zero spam complaints across all 3 campaigns — 0.00% across 2,983 sends. Domain reputation is healthy. This headroom must be protected by resolving unmailables before the next campaign.
